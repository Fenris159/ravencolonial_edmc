# Ravencolonial EDMC v1.8.6

## Carrier market cargo accounting

- Prevent squadron-carrier sales being counted twice when inventory arrives ahead of the matching market event. Keep separate bank movements, market trades, and explicit transfers distinct, including transfers containing multiple commodities. Prevent phantom cargo when a delayed purchase exceeds the carrier balance currently reported to Raven.
- Publish updated ship cargo after market purchases and sales on both carrier types and ordinary stations without waiting for another inventory event. Returning to a previously published amount still updates correctly when another cargo update is queued.
- Prefer complete main-ship Cargo.json matching the trade over EDMC cargo that has already been adjusted twice. Ignore unrelated or invalid files, and retain validated cargo when a same-second fallback is ambiguous.
- Keep unloaded startup inventory unknown until a complete hold is available. Exclude limpet service transactions and other known ship-only changes from carrier deliveries, including delayed callbacks while mixed inventory is held.
- Retain complete inventory intervals and deferred journal purchases until carrier stock is available. Release them after earlier updates finish only while the original physical evidence still supports the correction, preserving evidence timestamps and later ship changes. Use confirmed carrier stock for placement proofs; project linkage alone does not establish an empty manifest.
- Keep held withdrawals separate from older refinements and later known trades, so a held movement cannot hide a sale or create cargo. Preserve reported quantities across repeated rejected snapshots while keeping consumed quantities out of new or existing rollback adjustments. Retain delayed ship changes until their quantities have actually been reconciled, even when their timestamps precede a held inventory. Keep ambiguous same-second changes pending until their placement can be proved. Keep same-second limpet purchases after manual corrections from hiding later bank deposits.
- Preserve unresolved inventory evidence across restarts and commander changes. Ambiguous inventory history waits for a verified full carrier sync or manual correction instead of guessing a balance.
- Keep newer carrier syncs and manual corrections in order, and preserve trades arriving while a full manifest is being saved. Publish recovered carrier stock before releasing held movement. Late inventory cannot replay deliveries already covered by a correction.
- Serialize journal observation and dispatch with background reconciliation. Reserve stock for pending withdrawals and delayed cargo corrections even while acknowledgements arrive. Retry funded corrections after earlier deliveries finish, wait for earlier full carrier updates before retrying held movement, and prevent delayed opposing ship-only corrections from creating cargo at zero stock. Clear resolved or canceled correction evidence so later sessions can continue syncing.
- Retry failed recovery checkpoints even when no delivery is waiting to send, and retain ownership of full saves interrupted by a checkpoint failure. Preserve delivery identities across restart, retain confirmed evidence, and remove provisional records only after the originating commander lookup proves the carrier is unlinked.

Previously incorrect carrier balances still need a one-time manual correction.
